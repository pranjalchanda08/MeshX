meshx_err_t meshx_unpair_pre_reset_teardown ( void )

Execute mandatory Pre-Reset Lifecycle Teardown.

Dispatches targeted UNPAIR_REQUESTs to all active peers, followed by a broadcast of MESHX_SYS_NOTIFY_NODE_RESET to the Company Management Virtual / Group Address.

Address Selection & Audit Notes:

  • MESHX_MGMT_VIRTUAL_ADDR selects the destination address for the reset notification broadcast:
    • Intended Company Management Group Address: 0xCFFF (fixed group address range 0xC000..0xFEFF).
    • Virtual Address fallback: 0x8F42 (derived via code_gen.py from the management Label UUID per Bluetooth Mesh Profile §3.8.4 in the 0x8000..0xBFFF range).
  • All provisioned nodes belonging to the company network subscribe to this management address on boot.
  • Receiving nodes immediately cleanse stale link-table entries referring to the resetting node without waiting for offline heartbeat timeouts or unpair polling.
  • Stage 2 implements a 500ms network drain window before MCU reset/NVS erase, guaranteeing radio transmission queue flush even under mesh traffic contention.
