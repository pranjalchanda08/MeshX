## Round 2 Consensus — prior CRITICAL fixes are NOMINAL ONLY

Every reviewer concluded that the Round 1 remediations are surface-level and do not close the underlying corner cases. Cross-reviewer themes:

1. **Split-brain fencing (C3)** is cooperative and unauthenticated — `active_term` is carried in forwarding frames but not HMAC'd; no quorum; demotion is a soft "MUST demote within 100ms" with no enforcement.
2. **Bridge Cluster Key (C4)** lacks replay protection, commit-before-activate, revocation-erase, and per-bridge isolation.
3. **Transit whitelist (C1)** remains unsatisfiable for the forward multihop hop — no NAT binding for a transit source proxy, no defined transit-header creation.
4. **Two-tier NAT (R1)** re-opens truncation (Tier-2 window `0x7E00..0x7FFF` collides with Tier-1 slot 15 `0x7C00..0x7FFF`) and blows the 3.5 KB RAM budget (528 entries >> cap).
5. **Premature Stage 1 ACK (C2)** still silently loses unacknowledged-model traffic; `0xD7` NACK is fire-and-forget, unauthenticated, and has no defined multihop return path.
